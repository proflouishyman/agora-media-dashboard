About

How this dashboard works

A plain accounting of what this tool is, where its numbers come from, and what each view shows — so a reader can trust a count here as far as it actually goes, and no further.

The Media Monitor tracks press and social mentions of people affiliated with the SNF Agora Institute at Johns Hopkins University. It is an internal monitoring tool for the Institute's communications team, not a public metric of reach or influence — a mention logged here means the pipeline found and matched a document, nothing more, and a person with zero mentions logged is very likely still doing plenty of public-facing work the pipeline simply hasn't picked up yet.

Where the data comes from

Two feeds, kept honest on purpose

Every number on this site traces back to one of two sources.

The roster — who gets tracked at all — comes from the SNF Agora Institute's own public directory at snfagora.jhu.edu/directory: name, title, category (Core Faculty, Affiliated Faculty, Postdoctoral + Student Fellows, Visiting Fellows, Continuing Faculty + Fellows, Staff), profile link, and photo. It is re-pulled periodically so people who join or leave the Institute are picked up automatically, rather than relying on a hand-maintained list.

The mentions — every story, post, and citation — come from Meltwater, a commercial media-monitoring API, via a dedicated saved search built from that same roster (name variants OR'd together, required to co-occur with "Johns Hopkins", "Hopkins", "Agora", or "SNF"). Meltwater returns the raw hits; a filtering pass removes off-topic noise and syndication duplicates (the same wire story often runs under dozens of outlets in one pull) before anything is logged as a mention.

What "honest" means in practice here:

  • This is a backfilled snapshot, not a live daily feed — the pipeline has fetched a handful of times so far, and the timeline chart on the Overview page shades the days nothing was fetched rather than pretending they were quiet.
  • Sentiment is scored per mention of the person or the Institute, not per whole article, and most mentions today have no resolved sentiment at all — that "unresolved" share is always shown as its own honest bucket, never folded into "neutral" to make a chart look cleaner.
  • A person with no coverage logged is labeled "No coverage logged yet," never a fabricated zero-height bar or a hidden row.
  • A story missing a captured link or a shareable quote renders a plain "No link captured" note instead of a dead button.

Views

Six ways to look at the same underlying data

Nothing here is a second dataset — every view below reads the same five JSON exports, just sliced or scoped differently.

Overview

The institute-wide snapshot: headline stats, the mentions-per-day timeline (gaps and all), sentiment, today's repost pick, latest coverage, and the most-covered people this week.

People

The full tracked roster — all 100 people, filterable by category, split honestly into "Covered" and "Tracked, no coverage logged."

Stories

Every story the pipeline has logged, not just the latest teaser — filterable by scope, sentiment, and free-text search.

Trends

Week-over-week volume, reach, sentiment, source mix, and a person-by-week heatmap — the "is coverage changing" view.

Repost

Curated picks ready to share, plus the full archive of any shareable story, each with one-click X/Bluesky compose links and a copy button.

CES

The same institute-wide Overview, filtered to just the 17 people affiliated with the Center on Economy and Society — a scope, not a separate site.

Questions about a specific number, or a mention that looks wrong? This is an internal tool built and maintained for the Institute's communications team — flag it directly rather than treating anything here as a final public claim.
